HOW IT WORKS

GET ON THE BUS

Type your name and take a seat. The bus leaves Pettah and travels through Kegalle and Kandy to Deniyaya, then into the jungle village.

🎭2

SECRET ROLE

A black screen tells only you who you are: thief, police, doctor or villager.

🌙3

SURVIVE THE NIGHT

Thieves strike in the dark. The doctor protects, the police investigate. Elephants roam.

🗳️4

VOTE THEM OUT

Talk, accuse, vote. Catch every thief, or they take the village.

VILLAGE LIFE

Walk around, farm, trade and steal in real time. Thieves sneak out at night.

CLASSIC MAFIA

Turns with a narrator: night choices, morning news, discussion and the vote.
