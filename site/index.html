A DAY IN SAMS GAMA

How to play, in seven short chapters. Read it once, then go and catch a thief.

🚌The bus

Name your Damindu

Everyone in Sams Gama is a Damindu. Type your Damindu's name, shuffle or customise their look, and press PLAY. The 99 bus leaves Pettah with the music loud and a few parts falling off on the way to the village.

🎭The secret

A whisper in the dark

The screen goes black and tells only you who you really are: a villager, the police, the doctor, or one of the thieves. Keep a straight face. Nobody else knows.

🌾Daytime · 2 minutes

Work the village

Follow the yellow arrow. Draw water, buy your vegetable land (Rs 50) and a paddy plot (Rs 40), then harvest, fish from a boat, cook rice and curry and swap goods with neighbours. Picking from someone else's land costs Rs 10.

💰 Every morning you get Aswasuma: Rs 5 to 20.
🐝Trouble

Bees and elephants

Hunting in the jungle can upset a bees' nest. Run! Hide at any house, or lead them to your own house: they make a hive that guards it for two nights. Elephants left in the village eat crops fast by day. Chase them with the tractor or buy an electric fence.

🌙Night

Be in bed before dark

At dusk the arrow points home: press E to sleep. In the dark the thieves sneak out to rob houses and attack, the doctor guards, the police watch, and elephants trample anyone caught outside.

🔦 Outside at night everyone is a black shape with no name.
🪦Morning

The village news

The news tells who was robbed, who was framed and who did not wake up. The dead get a funeral, white flags at their house and a headstone at the kanatta. They come back as ghosts who can still help.

🔔The vote

Ring the bell

Ring the bell under the big tree to call a meeting. Talk, accuse, defend, vote. The one with the most votes leaves the village. Vote out every thief and the village wins.

WHO ARE YOU?

Your role is secret. Here is what each one does.

🧑‍🌾

VILLAGER

Find the thieves

Work, swap and stay alive. Watch who acts strange, then vote them out. Villagers never kill.

👮

POLICE

Question one a day

Ask one person each day: thief or not? You also have 3 bullets to scare off elephants. Use what you learn carefully.

🩺

DOCTOR

Save lives

Guard one person each night. Reach an injured player within 20 seconds and heal them, or they die.

🦹

THIEF

Don't get caught

Start with nothing. Each night the thieves can attack one person and rob sleeping houses. Tricks (T): hide stolen goods at someone's house, put mud in the well, put out the fire.

Who wins? Villagers win when every thief is voted out. Thieves win when they are as many as the villagers left, when their loot reaches the goal, or when they survive the final vote.

SURVIVAL GUIDE

Small things that keep your Damindu alive.

🍛Eat every day

Plain rice fills a little; add vegetables for rice and curry. Fish, meat, eggs and coconuts are good food. Empty food bar? Eat within 60 seconds or you die.

🏠Find your things

Your house, land and paddy have small icons in your colour, on the map (M) and in the village.

⚡Protect your crops

At your house, buy an electric fence (Rs 50) for your paddy or your land. Elephants get zapped.

🐝Keep the bees

Chased by bees? Run to your own house. The hive scares off elephants and stops thieves robbing you for two nights.

🚜The tractor

One for the whole village, 15 seconds a ride. Drive it into an elephant to chase it back to the jungle.

🌊Mind the water

Fall in and you have 30 seconds to climb out, or someone pulls you out. Take a boat from the jetty to fish.

🦹Stealing shows

A villager who steals at night gets a STOLE badge everyone can see. Only real thieves steal unseen.

👻Ghosts still play

Dead players fly as ghosts, do ghost jobs and paint whoever they think is a thief.

🧺Swap and queue

Walk up to someone to offer a swap. At the well and the cooking hut people wait in line, and anyone can jump the queue.
