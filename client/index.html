          THIEVES HIDING AMONG US!

          Police warn that one or more thieves will travel on today's bus to Berala Panathara, disguised as honest villagers. Nobody knows who they are.

          How to play

          • W A S D or arrow keys: walk. On a phone, drag the circle.
          • E: use the place you stand at (the yellow pointer).
          • R: your secret role's action. T: thieves' tricks.
          • F: eat. M: map (your house, land and paddy have icons).

          Land for sale!

          Everyone arrives with Rs 50. Buy the vegetable land next to your house (Rs 50) and one paddy plot (Rs 40) before you can grow anything. The owner's name goes on the sign. Harvested paddy grows back in 6 minutes.

          Aswasuma every morning

          When you wake up, every villager gets Aswasuma: Rs 5 to 20. Save it for land, or an electric fence (Rs 50) at your house to keep elephants off your paddy or vegetables.

          Eat or you will die

          Plain rice fills you only a little. Rice and curry, fish, meat (hunt in the jungle), eggs and coconuts are better. With an empty food bar you grow weak: eat within 60 seconds or die of hunger. Starving? Raw paddy or water fills 10%.

          Your secret role

          • Thief: starts with nothing; honest work gives nothing. One attack a night for all thieves together; robs houses and animals; plays tricks.
          • Police: questions one person a day, and has 3 bullets for elephants.
          • Doctor: guards one person a night, and heals the injured.
          • Villager: works, swaps goods, watches and votes. One villager can swim.

          Attacked? 20 seconds!

          A thief's attack leaves you injured on the ground. If the doctor reaches you within 20 seconds you are back in the game; if not, you die. The dead get white flags at their house, and birds eat their harvest.

          Night and elephants

          Go home and sleep before dark (E at your house). Elephants trample anyone outside at night, and everything you carry falls on the ground. The one tractor (15 seconds a ride) chases elephants back to the jungle.

          Watch out

          A villager who steals gets a STOLE badge. Thieves hide stolen goods at innocent houses, throw mud in the well or put out the cooking fire. Lines at the well and cooking hut: anyone can jump the queue.

          The village bell

          By day, ring the bell under the big tree (E) to call a meeting, once per match. Everyone votes, and the one with the most votes leaves. After the final day there is always a last vote.

          How to win

          Villagers: vote out every thief.

          Thieves: fill the loot bag, or outnumber the villagers, or survive the final vote.

          Out of the match? You become a ghost: fly around, do ghost jobs and paint the person you think is a thief. Ghosts cannot eat or vote.

                  How to play

                  A village with a secret. Up to 12 players ride the bus to Sams Gama. Most are honest villagers, but a few are secretly thieves. Nobody knows who.

                  Your village life

                  • Your house and colour. Your house, name tag and the ring under your feet are in your colour. On the map, your house 🏠, land 🥬 and paddy 🌾 have small icons.
                  • Buy first, then grow. You arrive with Rs 50 (thieves with nothing). Buy the vegetable land next to your house (Rs 50) and one paddy plot (Rs 40) of the six in the fields. Only then can you pick or harvest there. The sign shows the owner's name. Harvested paddy grows back in 6 minutes.
                  • Aswasuma. Every morning when you wake up, you get Aswasuma: Rs 5 to 20.
                  • Electric fence. At your house, upgrade with an electric fence (Rs 50) round your paddy plot or your vegetable land. Elephants get zapped and cannot eat it. Paddy an elephant eats takes 6 minutes to grow back.
                  • Eat every day. Plain rice (paddy, firewood, water at the cooking hut) fills you only a little; add vegetables for rice and curry. Fish, meat (hunt in the jungle), eggs and coconuts are good food. With an empty food bar you get weak (everyone can see it): eat within 60 seconds or you die of hunger. Starving with no food? You can chew raw paddy or drink water: it fills only 10%.
                  • Swap goods. Walk up to another player and offer a swap (milk for paddy, fish for vegetables…). They accept or say no.
                  • Lines. At the well and the cooking hut people wait in line, and anyone can jump the queue.
                  • Water. Fall in and you have 30 seconds to get out, or someone must pull you out. One villager can swim. Take a boat from the jetty to fish; press E next to land to get out.

                  Day and night

                  • Day (2 minutes): work, swap and watch what others do.
                  • Dusk: the arrow points home. Press E at your house to sleep before dark.
                  • Night: elephants roam and trample anyone outside; everything they carried falls on the ground for others to pick up. Everyone outside is a black shape with no name. Fireflies come out.
                  • The tractor: one for the whole village, 15 seconds a ride. Drive it into an elephant to chase it back to the jungle.
                  • Stealing: a villager who steals at night gets a STOLE badge everyone can see. Only the original thieves can steal without it.
                  • Death: a black screen and a sad violin. White flags go up at the dead person's house, and birds eat their harvest.

                  Secret roles online matches

                  • Villager: works, swaps and votes. Never kills.
                  • Thief: starts with nothing and gets nothing from honest work (it can only pretend). One attack a night for all the thieves together: the person is injured, not dead at once. Robs sleeping players' houses and takes animals for the secret loot. Tricks (T): hide stolen goods at someone's house, throw mud in the well, put out the cooking fire.
                  • Doctor: guards one player a night, and heals an injured player. Reach them within 20 seconds and they come back into the game; too late and they die.
                  • Police: questions one person a day (thief or not?) and has 3 bullets to shoot at elephants.
                  • Village meeting: by day, ring the bell under the big tree (E), once per match. Everyone votes; the one with the most votes leaves. After the final day there is always a final vote.
                  • Ghosts: killed, trampled, starved or voted out players become ghosts. They fly, do their own ghost jobs and paint the person they think is a thief. They cannot eat or vote.

                  Win: villagers win by voting out every original thief. Thieves win when they are as many as the villagers left, when their loot reaches the goal, or when they survive the final vote.

                  The host (who starts the bus) is the bus driver. Offline you practise alone with computer villagers. Follow the yellow arrow to do your first jobs.
